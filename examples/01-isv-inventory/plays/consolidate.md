## Sequence

| When | Channel | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Day 0 | Internal | Pull usage and contract terms for {product} at {customer}; confirm what {consolidate_onto} covers |
| Day 2 | Email | Send {exec_contact} a one-page consolidation brief: {product} (${annual_spend}/yr) vs what they already own |
| Day 5 | Meeting | Workshop with {business_owner} owner: must-have features, migration risks, change plan |
| Day 10 | Email | Fixed-fee migration SOW timed to the {renewal_date} renewal |

## Talk track (template)

- You're paying ${annual_spend} a year for {product}, and you already pay for {consolidate_onto}, which covers most of the same ground. Most of the work is change management, not technology.
- We'd time the cutover so you never pay for both after {renewal_date}.

## Discovery questions

1. Which teams would push back on moving off {product}, and why?
2. Which integrations or workflows depend on {product} today?
3. Is there a contractual notice we need to send, and by when?

## Objection handling

**"Users love the current tool."**  
We run a pilot with the most vocal team first and only proceed if adoption targets are met.

**"Migration will disrupt the business."**  
Fixed scope, phased by team, with a rollback plan, and finished before the renewal date so there's no double spend.
