## Talk track (template)

- You've got ${route_commit_remaining} left on your {marketplace_route} commitment and it ends {route_commit_end}. The {product} renewal is ${annual_spend} you're going to spend anyway.
- Buying it as a private offer on the marketplace keeps your pricing, can count toward the commitment, and puts it on the same bill.

## Discovery questions

1. Does your cloud agreement count marketplace purchases toward the commitment, and at what rate?
2. Who in procurement accepts marketplace private offers, and is a private marketplace enabled?
3. Are there other renewals before {route_commit_end} we should route the same way?

## Objection handling

**"Procurement has never bought through the marketplace."**  
We'll walk them through the first private offer. It's a click-to-accept order form against an existing vendor relationship.

**"Will the vendor's price change?"**  
We ask the vendor for a private offer at your current price or better. If it's worse, you renew direct as before.
