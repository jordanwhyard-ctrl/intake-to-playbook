## Talk track (template)

- You rated {product} {satisfaction}/5. Let's put {partner_alternatives} against your actual workflows, not a canned demo.
- We handle the vendor negotiation and the migration, so your team isn't running a project on top of their day job.

## Discovery questions

1. What would {product} have to fix for you to stay?
2. Who signs off, and what's the budget path for a switch?
3. What data and integrations have to move?

## Objection handling

**"Switching costs are too high."**  
We price the migration as a fixed fee and the partner often funds part of it. We'll show the 3-year total cost side by side.

**"We're locked in until renewal."**  
That's when we'd switch. We start now so the evaluation is done before the notice deadline.
