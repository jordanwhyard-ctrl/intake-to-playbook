## Discovery questions

1. When does your {current_tool} contract renew, and is there a notice period?
2. Which three workflows would you migrate first?
3. What would make switching not worth it?

## Objection handling

**"Migration is too risky."**  
We run both systems in parallel for 30 days and migrate the first five workflows ourselves at no cost.

**"Their pricing is lower."**  
Let's compare total cost, including admin time and add-ons. The ROI calculator usually closes that gap.
