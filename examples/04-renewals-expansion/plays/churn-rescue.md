## Talk track (template)

- We know the last quarter hasn't met the standard you signed up for. Here's what went wrong, what we've already fixed, and what's left with dates.
- Before we talk about renewal, I want to make sure the people using this every day are getting value.

## Discovery questions

1. Who is the new owner of this tool, and what does success look like for them?
2. If you could change one thing about the last 90 days, what would it be?
3. What would {competitor_mention} need to show you to win this?

## Objection handling

**"We're evaluating alternatives."**  
That's fair. Let's make sure the comparison includes migration cost and the {workflows} workflows already live. We'll also put the fix plan in writing.

**"We need to cut cost."**  
We can right-size to the seats you actually use and lock pricing for two years.
