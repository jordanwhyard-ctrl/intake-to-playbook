## Talk track (template)

- You have a deadline, so let's work backwards from it: onboarding takes about three weeks, and we'll start the security baseline in week one.
- Everything the insurer or auditor asked for is mapped to a line in the proposal, so you can forward it as evidence.

## Discovery questions

1. What exactly does the deadline require? Can you share the letter or questionnaire?
2. Who signs, and is there anyone else who needs to review?
3. Which systems would hurt most if they went down during cutover?

## Objection handling

**"Can you really onboard that fast?"**  
We reserve onboarding capacity the day we scope. Security controls go in first and the full transition follows.
